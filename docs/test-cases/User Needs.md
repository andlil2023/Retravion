## Deferred Verification

Released Product revision references and baseline-specific protection require separate verification when revision integration is designed and implemented. Stable Approved-version preservation is covered above and is not deferred to release integration. Centralized mapping, remote MCP, deletion, and ownership transfer are outside this increment. Exact transport, authentication mechanism and comparison semantics require implementation decisions; these cases do not claim those decisions or completed automation.
