# UN-AUDIT-01 — Traceability for Regulatory Compliance

**Need:** As a regulatory inspector, I need relevant application activity to be traceable, so that I can assess it during an inspection.

Direct inspector access to Retravion is not assumed; an administrator may present the information.

Business-record changes need attributable history that identifies the record and preserves the values established or changed. This is a cross-cutting expectation for future business-record features, not a claim that they already provide audit coverage. The Product increment covers creation, actual edits, retirement, reactivation, and unauthorized change attempts; historical gaps are not filled retrospectively.
